# Nomad long duration

Interpretation of the **jointly authored** method of Nick Sleep and Qais Zakaria
(Nomad Investment Partnership, Sep 2001–2014; approved IGY collection, Spring
2021). Joint material is attributed to both. Nothing is assigned to its
better-known member alone.

## When to use

- Judging whether scale is shared with customers (and reciprocated) or captured.
- Testing stakeholder generosity as a moat rather than margin leakage.
- Writing destination analysis: the terminal engine, not terminal noise.
- Holding concentrated positions through volatility with patient capital.

## Inputs required

- Unit economics showing scale effects + give-back evidence (prices, wages,
  reinvestment) over 5+ years.
- Stakeholder terms: customer value, employee pay vs rivals, supplier fairness.
- Partnership-style constraints: horizon, redemption terms, fee alignment.
  Without patience, this framework does not apply — say so.

## Workflow

1. Test scale-economies-shared (NOM-P01): does growth lower prices and invite
   reciprocation? Evidence in sales-per-foot, price gaps, volume loops.
2. Test stakeholder generosity (NOM-P02): fixed low markups, wage premiums as
   articles of faith; compute the robustness ratio where data allow.
3. Write destination analysis (NOM-P03): firms happiest to own "with no word
   for years" get the largest weights; mis-analysis is the only long-term risk.
4. Concentrate and stop transacting (NOM-P04) within patient-capital,
   fee-aligned, anti-scale structures (NOM-P05, P06); stay nomadic and absolute
   (NOM-P07); study honestly toward a terminal portfolio (NOM-P08).
5. Record joint attribution on every claim; first-person "I" in letters is
   still joint work per the collection's footnotes.

## Output contract

- Shared-scale verdict, generosity audit, destination statement, concentration
  rationale, invalidation (destination misread), missing information.
- Tag **Documented principle** / **Interpretation** / **Implementation choice**;
  source IDs from `references/sources.md`.
- Never present low margins as weakness here without running the margin-reframe
  (Heuristics in principles.md).

## Limitations

- Lock-up-like patience, small early base, global roaming, unlisted/prefs use,
  and freedom to close/return capital do not transfer to ordinary accounts.
- Late-period weights and Amazon timing vary by secondary compilation — ranges
  only; re-verify against Schedules of Investments / 13F-HR before quoting.
- Fee/hurdle percentages and AUM path are unconfirmed in fetched excerpts —
  mechanism described, numbers not encoded.
