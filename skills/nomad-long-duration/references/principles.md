# Nomad — operational principles

Joint attribution throughout. Labels: **Documented principle** /
**Interpretation** / **Implementation choice**.

## NOM-P01 — Scale-economies-shared: seek where size becomes the asset

- **Documented principle** (NOM-01, NOM-02): most companies pursue scale
  efficiencies; few share them. Sharing makes the model powerful. As the firm
  grows, savings return as lower prices; customers reciprocate with more
  volume, funding greater scale ("Yippee"). Exemplars jointly listed: Costco,
  Wal-Mart, Ryanair, AirAsia, GEICO, Nebraska Furniture Mart, Direct Line,
  Tesco, Carpetright, Amazon, parts of Berkshire.
- Procedure: (1) evidence the loop (scale → lower price → more volume →
  more scale) in unit data; (2) sales-per-foot vs run-of-mill; price gaps that
  compound (Sam's $1.4B price-match + $0.75B wage-match chasm framing).
- Contrary: scale captured as margin instead of shared — different (worse)
  business here.

## NOM-P02 — Stakeholder generosity as moat: low margins on purpose

- **Documented principle** (NOM-02, NOM-03): fixed low markups (14% branded /
  15% private-label at Costco; Sinegal refusing >14% on $22 jeans: "if I let
  you do it this time, you will do it again"); "grows through giving more
  back"; pay ~80% above rivals (Costco), lower prices as article of faith
  (Amazon). Robustness ratio (2005 interim): customer saving ~$5 per $1
  retained at Costco vs ~$1:$1 GEICO example — higher is harder to attack.
- Procedure: generosity audit (customer, employee, supplier); compute the
  ratio where data allow; treat cost rises as possible next-phase investment
  with dated proof, not automatic pardon.
- Margin-reframe heuristics (**Documented principle**, Costco 24× debate): low
  margin is the point; the firm could earn Wal-Mart margins at ~11× but would
  be a worse business.

## NOM-P03 — Destination analysis: terminal engine over terminal noise

- **Documented principle** (NOM-04): ask better questions to reach the firm's
  DNA; Conseco lessons kept them out of US banks; Stagecoach lessons kept them
  in Amazon (~$60M 2007 gain "return on prior losses"). "Only real long-term
  risk is mis-analysing destination." Highest confidence of favourable
  destination ("happily own with no word for years") = largest holdings.
- Procedure: write the destination in one paragraph; weight by destination
  confidence, not by near-term estimates.

## NOM-P04 — Concentration + inaction: sit on assets

- **Documented principle** (NOM-05): 18–25 names (2001–03) → 20 names with 10
  at ~80% → 10 companies end-2011 → essentially 3 at close. "Purchase/sale
  transactions all but ground to halt… considerable value added!" Two ways:
  buy cheap/sell/repeat (hundreds of decisions/yr) vs buy great at reasonable
  price and daily decide not to sell. Owning more stocks does not lower risk
  but raises it if knowledge is the edge. Walton/Gates did not diversify.
  "Make money sitting on assets" (Munger line reused, 2021 Preamble).
- **Implementation choice**: position caps and review dates from the mandate.

## NOM-P05 — Patient capital as edge: Partnership, not fund

- **Documented principle** (NOM-06): "aggregate patience" as the key advantage;
  genuinely long-term (few are!); only by looking further out than the
  short-term crowd can they be beaten. Five-year+ horizon required; 2009:
  no redemptions through ~70% drawdown-and-recovery — "trusting people not
  easy… results are yours as much as ours."
- Gate: without locked patience, do not apply this framework.

## NOM-P06 — Fee alignment + anti-scale: refundable, hurdle, closed by default

- **Documented principle** (NOM-07): refundable performance fee +
  time-dependent hurdle (report gross + guide net since inception varies by
  subscription date); no fee when no performance; drawdown refunds; closed by
  default past capacity, reopening only when prices create room; 2006 spin-out
  redemptions (~quarter over the concentration-limit rise) treated as filter.
- Mechanism described; percentages/AUM path unconfirmed — no numbers encoded.

## NOM-P07 — Nomadic, unconstrained, absolute-return value

- **Documented principle** (NOM-08): ~half real business value +
  owner-oriented management + long-term capital allocation — rare, hence global
  mandate, no obligation to invest when unfit. Breadth (Lucent prefs,
  Schibsted, unlisted Weetabix until policy change, Asian names, Costco) is why
  "Nomad." Absolute return; index only context; no leverage/shorts/derivatives
  ("old-fashioned contrarian stock picking… spend no time thinking about
  indices").
- **Implementation choice**: the mandate's universe replaces theirs.

## NOM-P08 — Honesty, study, mistakes: toward a terminal portfolio

- **Documented principle** (NOM-04, NOM-09): "super high-quality thinkers…
  intellectually honest, economically rational… out-think competition, allocate
  capital for years… shareholders could abdicate the right to trade…
  wonderful honestly-run compounding machines… terminal portfolio… do the work
  today so ready when at 50c." "We/I made lots of mistakes… model is learn…
  bear down on denial and ego." Fewer than 5% of listed firms do what is right
  vs what plays well; "vast majority of Nomad firms do."
- Procedure: mistake log with denial-check; multidiscliplinary reading toward
  the few names worth terminal holding.
