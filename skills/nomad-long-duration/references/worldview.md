# Nomad — worldview (Sleep and Zakaria, jointly)

**Interpretation** from `sources.md`. Every method claim below is joint work
unless marked post-Nomad solo activity.

## Partnership facts

- Launched early September 2001; investing from September 10th (Jan 2002
  letter). General Partner Marathon to Sep 2006, then Sleep, Zakaria and
  Company Ltd. Footnotes throughout: "Zak and I responsible for investment
  decisions since inception."
- Liquidated early 2014; cash returned. Dec 2013 letter did not know it was
  last. Jun 2014 Berkshire letter: "thirteen happy years… around $2bn made for
  clients, predominantly charities and endowments." Reasons jointly given:
  blunt regulation, unwillingness to justify by revolving door, process wrung
  dry ("unlikely to sell superb businesses to fund a Philippine cement
  company"), pull of independence, mid-40s, eating own cooking, recycling
  funds. Office kept; repurchased privately/for charities; continued to AGMs.
- Performance quoted only as ranges with basis labeled (gross/net, end-date
  Dec 2013 vs early 2014): commonly ~921% total vs ~117% MSCI World; ~20.8%
  p.a. before fees vs ~6.5%; ~18.4% net; $1 → ~$10.21 vs ~$2.17. Preamble 2021:
  "around twenty percent for twenty years" incl. post-Nomad private/charity
  compounding. Pick one basis per use.

## Values and habits

- Honesty, long-duration ownership, stakeholder generosity. "Businesses do the
  heavy lifting; we bring rationality if anything" (Jun 2014 letter).
- Concentrated few holdings; turnover to a halt; semi-annual letters (+ early
  Global Investment Reviews), later only "when meaningful to say"; deep
  reading; reverse-engineering others' holdings; holdings disclosed separately
  with lag, motive questioned.
- Multidisciplinary study: Cialdini, Attenborough/information-as-food,
  biology/symbiosis, equity yield curve — patience both increases and de-risks
  return because business outcomes are more predictable years out than near term.

## Uncertainty, fees, scale

- Cone of uncertainty wider for small holdings; mis-analysis the biggest risk;
  price volatility not risk — indeed the opposite. Refundable performance fee +
  time-dependent hurdle; "no performance fee when no performance"; drawdown
  refunds; "all yours in exchange for ~20bps" (2009). Closed by default from
  2004 past ~$100M after queue; reopened end-2008 only when prices created
  room. Scale deliberately capped.

## Evolution and tensions

- Cigar-butt → near-permanent holdings. Early (mid-2002): ~37% franchise, ~27%
  asset-backed, ~17% workouts at ~51c on the dollar. End: scale-shared
  ~45–60% mid-period → essentially 3 names (Amazon, Costco, Berkshire).
- Fee-rebate evolution and "and what then / X-amount": past X-amount, meaning
  is reinvesting in society — "world working properly." Only investment letters
  on a charity site: "why aren't there more?"
- Tensions: concentration vs redemption risk; disclosure vs second-guessing;
  global nomadism vs circle of competence; low margins looking "expensive" on
  P/E (answered by margin-reframe heuristics).

## Non-replicable advantages

Lock-up-like patience with no monthly reporting; small early base allowing
cigar-butts/Zimbabwe/Asia; freedom to roam, use unlisted/prefs, close and
return ~$3bn capacity in the mid-40s and compound privately/charitably for
20 years; founder-led cultures accessed early.
