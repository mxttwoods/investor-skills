# Buffett — operational principles

Translation pattern per principle: documented experience → value/lesson →
applicability → procedure → contrary evidence → decision implication →
evaluation. Labels: **Documented principle** / **Interpretation** /
**Implementation choice**.

## BUF-P01 — Stocks are businesses; Mr. Market serves, never guides

- **Documented principle** (BUF-02, BUF-03): judge by operating results, not by
  the daily quote; the manic-depressive partner offers prices to exploit or
  ignore; over the long run the market weighs business success.
- Experience: Graham tutelage; partnership-era arbitrage vs market noise.
- Applies to: operating equities, multi-year horizons. Not to trade timing.
- Inputs: 5–10 yr operating record; price history only as opportunity set.
- Procedure: (1) write the business result first; (2) check price second;
  (3) a price fall with intact operating milestones is patience-candidate, never
  automatic approval.
- Contrary evidence: sustained price fall anticipating operating break not yet
  in statements; verify with filings, not narrative.
- Invalidation: operating milestones miss for reasons outside temporary noise.
- Output: quality-first verdict; price treated as servant.
- Misapplications: "down = cheap"; ignoring multi-year relative underperformance
  of the operating thesis.
- Do not use when: the question is entry timing or stop discipline.

## BUF-P02 — Stay inside a defined circle of competence

- **Documented principle** (BUF-06): evaluate only selected businesses; knowing
  the perimeter matters more than its size.
- Applies to: all coverage decisions; gates every other principle.
- Procedure: (1) state what must be true to understand unit economics;
  (2) pass only with filed evidence; (3) else `insufficient-evidence` and stop.
- Invalidation: fast-change businesses where the perimeter moves yearly.
- Misapplications: stretching the circle because a price looks tempting (IBM
  precedent in cases.md).

## BUF-P03 — Prefer wonderful businesses with durable moats at sensible prices

- **Documented principle** (BUF-02, BUF-04): outstanding business at a sensible
  price over mediocre at a bargain; silk purses only; widen the moat (GEICO).
- **Interpretation**: moat = low cost, pricing power, habit, network, or
  regulation evidenced by margins + returns + reinvestment runway.
- Procedure: (1) classify quality; (2) evidence the moat in numbers and
  behavior (price rises without volume loss); (3) demand a sensible — not
 .css "steal" — price; (4) reject cigar-butts needing a workout to work.
- Contrary: moat erosion via foreign cost, technology shift, or regulation.
- Misapplications: paying wonderful-business multiples for merely good ones.

## BUF-P04 — Value by owner earnings and intrinsic value

- **Documented principle** (BUF-04, BUF-05): owner earnings = reported earnings
  + non-cash charges − average annual maintenance capex to hold competitive
  position/volume; intrinsic value = discounted future cash in minus out.
- Procedure: (1) compute owner earnings after stock comp (real cost);
  (2) discount with stated rate; (3) cross-check with reverse-DCF (what growth
  the price requires); (4) expose rate/growth/capex assumptions.
- **Implementation choice**: default discount 10%, terminal growth ≤3%,
  starting growth clamped and faded — user-configurable, never Buffett's.
- Invalidation: negative or maintenance-capex-unmeasurable economics.
- Misapplications: EBITDA or GAAP multiples as value; single-point fair values
  quoted with false precision.

## BUF-P05 — Demand margin of safety; be fearful when others are greedy

- **Documented principle** (BUF-04): no market prediction; epidemics of fear and
  greed are unpredictable; act inversely to sentiment only with business evidence.
- Procedure: (1) measure sentiment/proof of excess; (2) require larger discount
  for higher uncertainty; (3) reject "buy" above the computed buy-below band.
- **Implementation choice**: buy-below = most conservative fair-value view ×
  (1 − margin), margins rising with uncertainty (defaults 20/30/40/50/60%).
- Misapplications: contrarianism as a signal by itself.

## BUF-P06 — Count float and leverage costs honestly

- **Documented principle** (BUF-07): float is valuable only when its
  underwriting cost beats market funds; be volume-indifferent.
- For non-insurers, translate as: cost of every funding source (debt,
  leases, working-capital stretch) vs its return.
- Procedure: tabulate all funding costs; reject growth funded by dear money.
- Do not use for: non-financial operating quality directly.

## BUF-P07 — Retain $1 only if it makes ≥$1; report candidly

- **Documented principle** (BUF-05, BUF-08): partnership attitude; grow per-share
  intrinsic value; prefer unreportable earnings that compound to reportable ones
  that do not; add look-through retained earnings of investees less tax.
- Procedure: (1) test retention by market value created per $1 retained over
  5+ years; (2) check dilution, related-party dealings, and candor (promises vs
  results); (3) flag integrity concerns with investment implications.
- Misapplications: rewarding empire-building revenue growth.

## BUF-P08 — Concentrate, trade rarely, hold while the thesis compounds

- **Documented principle** (BUF-06): few securities dominate; snoring over
  activity; tax math favors the long hold of a compounder.
- **Implementation choice**: concentration limits, review cadence, and sell
  rules come from the mandate, not from Buffett.
- Procedure: record thesis + milestones + horizon at entry; on declines
  reassess business, financing, valuation, opportunity cost — then wait,
  investigate further, or revise.
- Invalidation: thesis milestones fail or a better risk-adjusted use of capital
  clears the bar after costs.
