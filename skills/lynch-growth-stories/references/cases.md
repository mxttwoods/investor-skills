# Lynch — cases

Profit figures below are as reported in secondary tables citing *Beating the
Street*; verify from filings before exact quotation.

## Illustrations

1. **Fannie Mae — largest reported profit (~$500M table).** Housing-affordability
   facts + long hold; Lynch's greatest single stock by his 1994 account, still
   his largest position then.
2. **Ford — cyclical/turnaround (~7×; ~$199M table).** Taurus/Sable line + cost
   cuts after the '81–82 recession: "terrible→mediocre→good" cycle buys, never
   low-P/E-at-peak buys.
3. **Chrysler — waited turnaround, twice.** Near-$2 deathbed early '80s; bought
   ~$4 after government-backed cash arrived (~10× vs 20× at max risk); biggest
   position Oct 1982; again ~$10 post-split Oct 1990 on packed-dealer evidence.
   Solvency gate over bottom-fishing (LYN-P08).
4. **Hanes L'Eggs — store check + homework.** Wife's test-market find;
   verified: weekly supermarket/drug visits vs 6-weekly dept-store; junk
   incumbent hosiery in food/drug; Hanes rack-jobbed sizes/fit, advertised fit
   not price. Rival test: bought 48 pairs of No Nonsense for office wear-test.
   Became largest position; bought out (Sara Lee); "might have been 30-bagger."
5. **Taco Bell / Dunkin' — scalable simplicity.** Packed units, 59-cent tacos,
   no Mexican-food competitor, multi-year hold to PepsiCo acquisition (multiple
   unverified — do not quote); Dunkin' understood coffee and recession-proof
   traffic ("10–15×" in his 1994 telling).
6. **Walmart — patience teaching example (not claimed core).** Public 1970;
   could wait 10 years verifying small-town replication, still ~30–35×.
   Microsoft 3-year wait → ~10× variant. Plenty of time, no rush (LYN-P06).

## Mistakes and misses

7. **Kaiser Industries + Polaroid.** Kaiser $26→$3 taught asset value
   (cash-generative subsidiaries, no debt → ~$50/share distributions);
   Polaroid $135→$18 taught the fallacies: "how much lower?", "it'll come
   back," "$3 — how much can I lose?" (loss is dollars, not price).
8. **100+ tenbaggers missed while running Magellan; Apple iPod / Nvidia later.**
   Listed 100+ ten-folders owned none, still did well — need few (LYN-P06).
   Saw the iPod, knew Apple's profit, didn't do the work: "how dumb was I?"
   Warns "going up" ≠ thesis; don't chase outside understanding.
