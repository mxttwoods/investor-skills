# Lynch — operational principles

Labels: **Documented principle** / **Interpretation** / **Implementation choice**.

## LYN-P01 — Know what you own: the two-minute story

- **Documented principle** (LYN-01, LYN-02): explain in ≤2 minutes to a child
  why you own it; simple, narrow, understandable businesses preferred;
  incomprehensible tech rejected.
- Procedure: `thesis_2min` = what it does + why earnings grow + risks/catalyst.
  "Going up" fails. Re-write on every material event.
- Misapplications: buying the product instead of the business.

## LYN-P02 — Classify first: six categories, different tests

- **Documented principle** (LYN-01): slow growers, stalwarts, fast growers,
  cyclicals, turnarounds, asset plays. Category dictates expectations and which
  numbers matter (fast growers ~20–25% ideal vs stalwarts ~10–12% vs slow
  growers dividend-only — Lynch's stated bands, not universal law).
- Procedure: force `category` before valuation; apply the category checklist
  (growth tests for growers; cycle-position tests for cyclicals; solvency tests
  for turnarounds; sum-of-parts for asset plays).
- Misapplications: valuing a cyclical on peak earnings as if stalwart.

## LYN-P03 — Edge-first ideas, verification after

- **Documented principle** (LYN-02, LYN-03): start from job, shopping, dealers,
  suppliers, customers — then verify. Observation is a lead, never a thesis.
- Procedure: split `idea_source (observed)` vs `verification (filings)`;
  ban tip-on-bus buys; require filed confirmation of unit economics.
- Examples in cases.md (Hanes, Taco Bell, Dunkin') illustrate, never prove.

## LYN-P04 — GARP: PEG plus the famous numbers

- **Documented principle** (LYN-01): fair P/E ≈ growth rate; PEG<1 interesting;
  plus P/E vs history/industry, earnings consistency, D/E, cash floor, insider
  buying, buybacks, inventory vs sales, pension/cash-flow checks.
- **Interpretation**: compute PEG, D/E, cash/market-cap, inventory trend with
  category-adjusted thresholds.
- **Implementation choice**: exact cutoffs are user parameters, not Lynch's.
- Contrary: PEG>1 with accelerating verified reinvestment runway can still pass
  with written justification.
- Misapplications: rigid secondary screens (e.g. P/E<40) quoted as Lynch's.

## LYN-P05 — Prefer boring/niche/unloved; avoid hot, whisper, diworsified

- **Documented principle** (LYN-01, LYN-04): love dull, simple, niche,
  spin-offs, out-of-favor industries. Avoid hot-industry hot stocks, $3
  "how-much-can-I-lose," "down from $100 so cheap," long shots (0-for-30 by his
  count), whisper stocks, unrelated-M&A diworsification (original meaning).
- Procedure: blocklist check + `boring_score` note; flag unrelated acquisition
  as thesis-break until proven otherwise.

## LYN-P06 — Tenbagger math: let winners run; 6/10 is great

- **Documented principle** (LYN-02, LYN-03): a few big winners pay for many
  flats; right 6/10 is excellent, never 9/10; don't water weeds (add to
  deteriorating) and cut flowers (sell winners early); best holds measured in
  years.
- Procedure: size so one winner matters; add only on confirming fundamentals;
  log early sales as regret cases.
- Misapplications: concentrated "Lynch" portfolios of 10–15 cited from
  secondary inference — Lynch ran ~1,400 names at peak; focus limits come from
  the mandate.

## LYN-P07 — Anti-timing: stomach over brain

- **Documented principle** (LYN-02): no one predicts market/rates/economy
  consistently; corrections are normal and frequent; require a decline plan,
  not a forecast.
- Procedure: forbid macro vetoes; pre-commit add-on-positive-fundamentals
  rules and cash needs before drawdowns.

## LYN-P08 — Turnaround solvency first + recheck/sell discipline

- **Documented principle** (LYN-01, LYN-04): cash to survive, debt vs assets,
  what fixed the problem, catalyst, costs cut, what survives bankruptcy. If
  fundamentals slip, say goodbye. Second/third turnarounds (Chrysler ×2) only
  on fresh solvency proof.
- Procedure: solvency gate before earnings story; scheduled story recheck;
  sell triggers written at entry.
