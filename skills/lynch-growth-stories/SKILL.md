# Lynch growth stories

Interpretation of Peter Lynch's documented method (Magellan 1977–90; *One Up on
Wall Street*, 1989; *Beating the Street*, 1993). It connects theses to company
fundamentals; it does not bless familiar products as investments.

## When to use

- Classifying a business before valuing it (category dictates the test).
- Writing and rechecking the two-minute story against earnings, debt, cash.
- Screening GARP-style with PEG plus balance-sheet and inventory checks.
- Deciding to hold a winner, add on strength, or say goodbye on deterioration.

## Inputs required

- Category hypothesis + two-minute story (what it does, why earnings grow,
  risks/catalysts) in plain words a child could follow.
- Filed numbers: P/E vs history/industry, growth, D/E, cash, insider
  buying, buybacks, inventory vs sales trend.
- Idea source labeled `observed` (job, store, supplier) vs `verified` (filings).
  Observations are leads only.

## Workflow

1. Assign one of six categories (LYN-P02) before any valuation.
2. Write the two-minute story (LYN-P01). "Going up" is not a reason.
3. Run PEG + famous numbers (LYN-P04); solvency gate first for turnarounds
   (LYN-P08).
4. Check the boring/niche/unloved vs hot/whisper/diworsified screens (LYN-P05).
5. Set the hold/sell rule at entry: hold while the story is intact (LYN-P06),
   never time the market (LYN-P07); recheck on schedule and sell on
   fundamental slip.

## Output contract

- Category, story, numbers table, verification status, hold/sell conditions,
  invalidation triggers. Label **Documented principle** / **Interpretation** /
  **Implementation choice**; source IDs from `references/sources.md`.
- "Buy what you know" means verify what you observe — never a familiar-product
  buy without filings work.

## Limitations

- Magellan-era advantages (small start, Fidelity access, analyst network,
  1980s flows, 8,000-name opportunity set) do not transfer; today's public
  count is far smaller.
- Later PEG-screen popularizations (e.g. rigid P/E<40 rules) are secondary and
  not encoded as Lynch's.
- Yearly Magellan return series and profit tables need filing verification
  before exact quotation.
