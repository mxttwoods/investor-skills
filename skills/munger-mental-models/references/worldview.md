# Munger — worldview

**Interpretation** from `sources.md`. Kept where it explains the decision process.

## Formative

- Omaha 1924; Buffett & Son grocery work; Caltech meteorology (WWII), Harvard
  Law; Munger, Tolles & Olson (1962); Wheeler, Munger & Co. (1962–75); met
  Buffett 1959; Berkshire vice-chair 1978; Wesco chair/CEO 1984–2011; Daily
  Journal chair 1977–2022; Costco board 1997–2023.
- Division of labor with Buffett over ~60 years: Munger as architect of the
  shift from cigar-butts to great businesses at fair prices (Buffett 2023
  tribute). **Documented principle** context.

## Influences (documented)

- Franklin: deserve what you want; deliver what you would buy; persuasion via
  interest. Darwin: prioritize disconfirming evidence. Jacobi (mathematics):
  invert. Physics/engineering: scale, breakpoints, backup systems, critical
  mass. Grandfather Judge Munger / Epictetus / Housman: anticipate trouble,
  underspend, mischance as material to behave well. Confucius: value transfer.
- Cialdini-psychology link is often claimed in secondary writing — **gap**,
  no primary confirmation encoded.

## Stated values and habits

- Latticework: ~80–90 big ideas, a handful carry the freight; isolated facts
  are unusable unless hung on theories (1994). **Documented principle.**
- "Safest way to get what you want is to deserve it"; wisdom acquisition as
  moral duty; go to bed a little wiser; seamless web of deserved trust.
- Assiduity + intense interest required; put practice with the most apt
  learning machines.
- Checklist routines; Darwinian disconfirmation; two-track analysis (rational
  interests + subconscious misfunctions). **Documented principle** (2007, 1995).

## Attitudes

- Uncertainty: no sure things; avoid leverage, crazies, crooks; anticipate
  trouble without unhappiness. Envy: the stupid sin with no fun; caring that
  someone makes money faster is deadly (2003 Wesco notes). Self-pity near
  paranoia; ideology cabbages the mind; sloth/unreliability guarantee failure.
- Loss: deprival super-reaction makes losses and near-misses irrational (1995)
  — design against it in advance.

## Evolution and tensions

- Still allowed occasional Graham-style cheap buys while teaching "few big
  bets, rarely sell" — tension recorded, not smoothed.
- China optimism (better 20-yr prospects, cheaper leaders) vs the Alibaba
  regulatory miss he owned as a mistake (2023).
- Late-cycle acknowledgments (Apple/Japan "no-brainer" framing) vs decades of
  tech-circle avoidance.

## Non-replicable advantages

60-year Buffett partnership, permanent float, no forced selling, reputation
deal flow (e.g. Li Lu), board seats/information, starting capital and time
horizon. The skill encodes the checklists, not the access.
