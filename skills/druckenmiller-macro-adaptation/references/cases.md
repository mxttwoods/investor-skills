# Druckenmiller — cases

Sizing figures vary by retelling; ranges given, points avoided. Filings are
snapshots, never the full exposure or reasoning.

1. **1981–82 long 30-yr Treasuries (~14%).** New Duquesne capital, ~50% in one
   bond bet that Volcker would crush ~12% inflation despite the equity bear.
   Up strongly "right out of the chute." Concentration + policy-error reading.
2. **Oct 1987 crash reversal.** Long into Friday Oct 16; studied charts over the
   weekend; sold into the Monday bounce. Dow −22% Oct 19; Duquesne ended 1987
   strongly up (exact figure varies — not quoted). Flexibility over ego.
3. **Sep 1992 short GBP / long DM (Quantum).** ~$1.5B starter Aug 1992 on ERM
   misalignment (German reunification vs UK housing recession); added after the
   Schlesinger editorial and lira success; Soros pushed "for the jugular"
   (notional cited $5.5–15B by retelling — range only). UK exited the ERM Sep
   16 (Black Wednesday); fund up strongly in 1992 (~$1B on sterling alone plus
   gilts/OATs/equities per reports). Druckenmiller architect/PM, Soros sizer —
   roles preserved, not merged.
4. **1999–2000 tech whipsaw (Quantum exit).** Short ~$200M internet Feb 1999 →
   ~$600M loss by mid-March; flipped long that summer → end-1999 strongly up
   after being down; sold Jan 2000, re-bought ~$6B Mar 2000 near the top →
   ~$3B six-week loss; left Soros Apr 2000 (NYT/WSJ/BBC contemporaneous).
   Self-verdict: knew the rule, could not control the emotion. Core DRU-P05
   teaching case.
5. **2005–08 subprime short then commodity trend.** Subprime calendar from May
   2005 → 2006 warning; short into 2007; long commodities early 2008 on charts
   + expected liquidity despite bearish economy; exited May–Jun 2008 on chart
   break. 2008 result strongly positive vs hedge-fund average (press-reported;
   exact positions fragmentary — gap).
6. **Dec 2018–May 2019 flip.** Years urging normalization; Dec 2018 pause call
   with Warsh; May 2019 tariff shock: 93% long → net flat + Treasuries.
   Pre-committed reversal logic executed.
7. **2018–19 Microsoft/cloud concentration.** Q4 2018 13F: Microsoft >27% of
   Duquesne — cloud-disruptive + defensive concentration example (filing-based,
   secondary reporting).
8. **Fall 2022–Mar 2024 Nvidia AI chain.** Junior partner Fall 2022 → starter
   pre-ChatGPT → large; AI "as real as the internet" (Sohn May 2023); trimmed
   late Mar 2024 on short-term overhype / long-term underhype; regret voiced at
   higher prints. Plus Microsoft/Alphabet/TSMC/Broadcom chain. Sensor-network +
   rhythm-sizing exhibit.
