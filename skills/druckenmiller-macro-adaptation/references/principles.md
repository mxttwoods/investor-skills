# Druckenmiller — operational principles

Labels: **Documented principle** / **Interpretation** / **Implementation choice**.

## DRU-P01 — Liquidity moves markets, not earnings or valuation

- **Documented principle** (DRU-01): Drelles lesson, repeated 2015–2024 —
  central banks/credit/financial conditions first for market direction;
  earnings for stock selection and confirmation only.
- Procedure: regime diagnosis starts with policy + credit + conditions; never
  with a valuation screen.
- Misapplications: using liquidity as a permanently-bullish excuse.

## DRU-P02 — Never invest in the present; visualize ~18 months ahead

- **Documented principle** (DRU-02): 18 months is a heuristic for forcing a
  future state, not a rigid forecast horizon.
- Procedure: write the future state, what price does there, and the chain that
  gets there. No future state = no trade.

## DRU-P03 — Concentration with conviction; sizing is the return driver

- **Documented principle** (DRU-03): 50%-in-one-bond-bet precedent (1981);
  Soros would have done more; fellow concentrators cited; sizing most of the
  equation (2009).
- **Implementation choice**: caps, leverage bans, and short bans come from the
  mandate — this long-only skill sizes within them.
- Procedure: if edge + asymmetry are not both clear, default small; adding
  names dilutes.

## DRU-P04 — Only asymmetric, liquid, cheap-to-be-wrong bets; invest then investigate

- **Documented principle** (DRU-04): 1992 GBP — ~0.5% six-month carry to be
  wrong vs 15–20% devaluation gain; liquid exit (sterling) as precondition;
  starter then add on confirmation.
- Procedure: asymmetry table (hold-cost vs break-gain); liquidity/exit check;
  staged entry with confirmation triggers.

## DRU-P05 — Flexibility over ego: reverse fast, admit emotional error

- **Documented principle** (DRU-05): knew tech was overvalued by Dec 1999 yet
  overplayed (short → stopped → long → $6B top → ~$3B six-week loss, left
  Soros Apr 2000); long pre-1987 Friday, reversed Monday; 93%-long to flat +
  Treasuries on the May 2019 tariff shock.
- Procedure: exit discipline separate from entry conviction; early/wrong = cut,
  even after a recent win. State the reversal trigger at entry.

## DRU-P06 — Multi-asset menu + bottom-up + technicals for timing

- **Documented principle** (DRU-06): earnings for direction candidates,
  liquidity + technicals for timing, valuation only for distance; menu
  (bonds/currencies/commodities) lets the best expression be picked, many of
  which rally when equities fall; macro confirmed via companies + price trend.
- Procedure: pick the cleanest expression; confirm with anecdote + trend; exact
  chart rules undisclosed — describe the observed confirmation, invent none.

## DRU-P07 — Reflexivity/positioning: trade the policy error and the crowd

- **Documented principle** (DRU-07): ~80% of big bear-market money from
  central-bank mistakes; poster children 2003–04 1% + "considerable period";
  QT/corporate-debt/breadth (2019); fiscal horror (2023). Second-order: map who
  must act, the political constraint, consensus assumptions, and follow-on
  circles (devaluation → gilts/equities up, not down).
- Procedure: stakeholder-action map + consensus-assumption list before sizing.

## DRU-P08 — Adapt the style; outsource edge to youth; rhythm-size

- **Documented principle** (DRU-08): Nvidia chain 2022–24 via junior sensor;
  no stops but rhythm-sizing (bigger in rhythm, low gross when unanalyzable
  post-intervention); 2017 self-described terrible macro year saved by equities.
- Procedure: when government dominates or confusion is high, reduce gross and
  shorten horizon by rule (**Implementation choice**: thresholds from mandate).
