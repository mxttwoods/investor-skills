# Druckenmiller macro adaptation

Interpretation of Stanley Druckenmiller's documented method (Duquesne 1981–2010
as principal record; Quantum 1988–2000; family office after). Tactical and
short-horizon by design — kept separate from long-term business analysis.

## When to use

- Regime change or central-bank/fiscal development that reprices liquidity.
- Concentrated, asymmetric, liquid expressions with defined exits.
- Reversing or resizing when price, positioning, or policy proves the thesis wrong.
- Timing questions a business-quality conclusion cannot answer.

Do NOT use for multi-year business quality verdicts — that is Buffett/Nomad
territory. A business-quality conclusion never implies an entry signal, stop,
or size here.

## Inputs required

- Future state 12–18 months out (heuristic, not forecast): what happens, what
  price does there, what policy error/crowd behavior drives it.
- Asymmetry math: max loss if wrong (carry/cost to hold) vs gain if right;
  liquidity/exit check.
- Company-anecdote + price-trend confirmation (bottom-up + technicals), not
  economist forecasts.
- Current gross exposure and confusion level (reduce/shorten when government
  dominates or the picture is unanalyzable).

## Workflow

1. State the regime and the policy error/crowd behind it (DRU-P01, P07).
2. Visualize 18 months ahead, not the present (DRU-P02).
3. Size only on conviction + asymmetry in liquid instruments; start small, add
   on confirmation (DRU-P03, P04).
4. Express in the best asset (rates/FX/commodities/equities) with bottom-up and
   chart confirmation (DRU-P06).
5. Reverse fast on emotional or evidentiary error; being early = being wrong
   until fixed (DRU-P05). Play bigger in rhythm, smaller in confusion (DRU-P08).

## Output contract

- Regime call, future-state sketch, asymmetry table, expression + exit levels,
  invalidation triggers, review date. Tag **Documented principle** /
  **Interpretation** / **Implementation choice**; source IDs in references.
- Distinguish the tactical conclusion from any long-term business view.

## Limitations

- Freedom to use leverage, shorts, any asset, and 100%+ sizing does not
  transfer; mandate constraints bind here hardest.
- Exact chart rules were never disclosed — no invented technical system.
- Quantum sizing varies by retelling ($5.5–15B notional); quote ranges, not
  points. Post-2010 performance is undisclosed; 13Fs are snapshots only.
- 2008 sequence and 2016–19 whipsaw details are fragmentary — recorded as gaps.
