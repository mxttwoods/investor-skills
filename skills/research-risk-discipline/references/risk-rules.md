# Risk rules

**Implementation choices** (defaults; mandate overrides). Not doctrines.

- Research never grants order permission. No auto-execution, no implied fills.
- Long-only; no leverage, shorts, or options in this mandate's analyses.
- Single-name cap default 10%; 10–20 names; review concentration, correlation
  with holdings, ADR/foreign exposure on every idea.
- Stale holdings disclosures: always show reporting date, publication date,
  and limits (partial exposure, no private reasoning). Never present as current
  recommendations.
- Business-quality conclusions never imply entry/stop/size. Trade timing needs
  a separate tactical analysis (Druckenmiller + Marks) with its own triggers.
- Quantitative rules: expose formulas/units/periods/assumptions/sensitivities;
  ranges over points; normalize cyclicals (multi-year average margins) before
  EV/EBIT conclusions; convert currencies and include minority interest before
  dividing by market cap.
- Precision rule: match significant figures to evidence strength; flag estimates.
