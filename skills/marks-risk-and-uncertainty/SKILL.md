# Marks risk and uncertainty

Interpretation of Howard Marks's documented method (Oaktree memos from 1990;
*The Most Important Thing*, 2011; *Mastering the Market Cycle*, 2018). It sets
the risk frame before any valuation; it never produces price targets.

## When to use

- Asking what the price already assumes and why consensus may be wrong.
- Judging where we stand in a cycle vs predicting where we go.
- Separating permanent-loss risk from volatility.
- Setting defensive vs aggressive posture at extremes only.

## Inputs required

- Price-implied expectations (reverse-DCF or comparables-implied growth).
- Consensus view in one paragraph + credit/market temperature evidence.
- Candidate's downside: how much is lost if the bear case is right, and how
  bad must things get (Klarman-adjacent question answered with Marks tools).
- `insufficient-evidence` permitted when expectations cannot be read.

## Workflow

1. Define risk as permanent loss, not volatility (MRK-P01). List its forms here.
2. Do second-level thinking (MRK-P02): consensus → priced-in → where it is
   wrong → what instead. Contrarianism is not automatic divergence.
3. Locate the pendulum (MRK-P03/P06): psychology, credit availability, price,
   prospective return. Act only at compelling extremes; otherwise hold posture.
4. State price vs value (MRK-P04) with the magnetic-pull timing caveat.
5. Refuse single-scenario macro forecasts (MRK-P05); use neutral assumptions +
   margin of safety (MRK-P08). Fish in inefficient ponds by specialization.
6. Asymmetry first: avoid losers, then seek winners (MRK-P07).

## Output contract

- Expectations statement, cycle location with evidence, downside-first verdict,
  posture recommendation, invalidation conditions, missing information.
- Tag **Documented principle** / **Interpretation** / **Implementation choice**;
  source IDs from `references/sources.md`.
- No macro point forecast; no probability quoted without a defined method.

## Limitations

- "Five market calls in 50 years" were posture tilts at extremes while staying
  invested — not timing trades; do not encode as a timing system.
- Sea Change (Dec 2022) is his self-flagged exception to the no-macro rule.
- Distressed-scale advantages (closed-end billions deployed weekly in 2008) do
  not transfer to an ordinary account.
