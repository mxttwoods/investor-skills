# Marks — cases

## Illustrations

1. **High-yield entry, 1978–85.** Asked to "figure Milken in California";
   learned any asset can be over/underpriced regardless of quality: "intelligent
   bearing of risk for profit."
2. **Nifty Fifty, 1969–74.** "No price too high" → devastating losses in the
   best companies. Origin of MRK-P04.
3. **TCW distressed 1988; Oaktree 1995.** First distressed funds at a major
   institution; left when fee-sharing was refused. Specialization/inefficiency
   institutionalized as the six tenets.
4. **bubble.com, Jan 2000.** TMT at multiples of revenue or no revenue; owed
   skepticism despite "standing in front of a freight train." S&P −46%,
   Nasdaq −80% into 2002.
5. **2004–07 caution + reserve.** Low prospective returns (2004) → housing
   fallacy (2005) → thinnest premia ever (Jul 2007); sold up, raised the bar,
   raised ~$11B VIIb Jan 07–Mar 08. Early, then vindicated (−57% bear).
6. **Sep–Oct 2008 buying.** "Nobody Knows" binary (if not world-end, act) +
   "Limits to Negativism" third-stagebear call; ~$6B in a quarter (~$400M/week)
   in senior debt while few others bought. Posture tilt, not prediction.
7. **Mar 2012 Déjà Vu; Mar 2020 Nobody Knows II.** 2012 contrarian reread of
   1979 "Death of Equities"; 2020 facts/extrapolation/speculation split —
   "spend some, not all/none" into forced selling. 2012–21 S&P ~16.5%/yr after.
8. **Sea Change, Dec 2022.** Claimed third sea change (risk/return mindset
   mid-70s; 40-yr declining rates from Volcker; then 0–2% → 2–4% base rates;
   HY ~4–5% → ~8% equity-like contractual returns). Self-flagged exception to
   the no-macro rule — "observing/inferring, not predicting."

## Mistakes and humility exhibits

- Too-early caution 2004–05 ("complaining too soon"); missed the 1979 call for
  lack of scars; knee-jerk skepticism corrected by his son (2021); Krugman/Fed
  humility exhibits quoted approvingly. Few single-name errors disclosed — gap
  recorded in sources.md.
- "Nobody" predicted the post-Mar-2020 torrid rally's speed — limits owned.
