# Marks — worldview

**Interpretation** from `sources.md`.

## Formative

- Wharton BSE Finance 1967; Chicago MBA (Accounting/Marketing) 1969; CFA 1975.
- Citicorp 1969–78 (equity research → Director of Research); 1978–85 high-yield
  / convertibles under the Milken sea change; TCW distressed from 1988; Oaktree
  founded 1995 with Karsh and partners after TCW refused fee-sharing.
- Nifty Fifty 1969–74 (>90% losses holding the best companies) as the origin of
  the price/value obsession. Chicago taught DCF + EMH he now qualifies.

## Influences (his words)

Galbraith, Charlie Ellis, Taleb, Milken, Graham, Buffett — "glean snippets…
assemble own formula. Can't copy" (2013). Plus Keynes (beauty contest; "market
can remain irrational longer than you can remain solvent"), Ellis's Loser's
Game (1975), Graham's voting/weighing machine, Buffett's important+knowable
filter, Housel/Ferguson on stationarity and complexity. Taleb/Kahneman depth
beyond citation is a **gap**.

## Stated values and habits

- Memo writing since Oct 1990 as a thinking tool ("writing tightens thinking").
- Primacy of risk control + consistency (Oaktree tenets 1–2); inefficiency,
  specialization, no style drift, macro-forecasting not critical, disavowal of
  market timing (tenets 3–6).
- Temperature-taking; neutral assumptions; margin of safety; "I don't know" as
  a complete answer; "we never bet the ranch."
- Dare to be different, wrong, and look wrong (2006/2014) — non-consensus
  posture with humility about frequency.

## Attitudes

- Risk/uncertainty: respect randomness and distributions, not point forecasts;
  risk is hidden, deceptive, multi-faceted (24 forms), shows only when tested.
- Loss: survival under negatives beats maximizing under favorables; selling at
  the bottom is the cardinal sin; buying at the top is fluctuation.
- Forecasting: agnosticism wiser than self-delusion; the industry persists via
  cognitive dissonance and self-justification (Mistakes Were Made citation).

## Evolution and tensions

- Equity-quality → credit risk/return mindset (mid-70s); risk memos
  2006→2014→2015; book codification 2011→2018; Sea Change 2022 as the sole
  claimed macro-regime deviation ("observing/inferring, not predicting").
- Tensions honestly kept: "don't forecast / don't time" vs five posture calls +
  Sea Change (resolution: tilt only at extremes, stay invested); bottom-up vs
  where-we-stand judgments that embed macro; Chicago EMH training vs
  inefficiency exploitation; risk primacy vs the need for winners.

## Non-replicable advantages

$11B distressed reserve + $400M/week buying Sep–Dec 2008 requires locked
closed-end capital; senior debt of quality buyouts at cents on prior equity;
scale for hung bridges; mandate to stay invested; deal-flow reputation. The
skill encodes posture logic, not purchasing power.
