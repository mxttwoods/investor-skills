# Router — selection matrix

Rows: decision types. Columns: frameworks (B=Buffett, M=Munger, L=Lynch,
K=Marks, D=Druckenmiller, N=Nomad, S=shared discipline always on).

| Decision | B | M | L | K | D | N | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Strong business, excessive price | ★ | ★ | · | ★ | · | ★ | Patience test + expectations + destination; usually reject-for-mandate at this price |
| Cheap, deteriorating economics | · | ★ | ★ | ★ | · | · | Loss-first + category check; cigar-butt screen |
| Growth story, weak financials | · | ★ | ★ | ★ | · | · | Story-vs-numbers; base rates; consensus check |
| Falling price, intact operating thesis | ★ | ★ | ★ | ★ | ○ | ○ | Patience vs trap; recheck story; expectations reset |
| Rising price, deteriorating thesis | ★ | ★ | ★ | ★ | ○ | · | Sell-discipline; flush corroboration, not thesis |
| Missing/contradictory financials | · | ★ | · | ★ | · | · | Too-hard / insufficient-evidence allowed |
| Stale holdings as "recommendation" | · | · | · | · | · | · | Reject premise; show dates/limits (S rules) |
| Long vs tactical conflict | ★ | · | · | ★ | ★ | ★ | Separate the horizons; no timing from quality views |
| Concentration/leverage/liquidity bind | · | ★ | · | ★ | ★ | · | Risk-rules gate; size within mandate |
| New evidence reversing assessment | ★ | ★ | ★ | ★ | ★ | ★ | Re-route; state what changed and what would change back |
| Persuasive but unsourced quotation | · | ★ | · | · | · | · | Attribution check; no doctrine from quotes |
| Irrelevant to all frameworks (e.g. option-expiry microstructure) | — | — | — | — | — | — | State irrelevance; do not force a fit |

★ primary · supporting ○ sometimes (tactical leg) — selection reasons recorded per task.
