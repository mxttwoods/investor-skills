# Router demo — quality says yes, timing says wait

Synthetic company. Demonstrates selection, inspectability, and a preserved
long-vs-tactical disagreement. Not investment advice; teaching trace only.

## Question

"Juniper Field Systems (fictional): industrial sensors, 28% ROIC 5-yr,
net cash, founder-CEO, 14% growth, 22× owner earnings after a −18% drawdown
on a soft quarter. 4-year horizon, long-only $10–20k mandate. Buy now?"

## 1. Router identification

- Decision: single-stock quality + entry timing. Asset: US equity.
- Horizons: 4-yr business view AND weeks–months tactical entry.
- Evidence: 5-yr statements (synthetic), soft quarter call, liquidity backdrop
  tightening, chart soft. No holdings-disclosure issues.

## 2. Selection

| Framework | Selected | Why |
|---|---|---|
| buffett-business-quality | yes | moat/owner-earnings/patience question |
| munger-mental-models | yes | invert + incentives before deciding |
| lynch-growth-stories | yes | classify (stalwart) + story recheck |
| marks-risk-and-uncertainty | yes | expectations reset + posture |
| druckenmiller-macro-adaptation | yes, tactical leg only | liquidity squeeze + entry timing |
| nomad-long-duration | no | no shared-scale/reciprocation evidence; reason recorded |
| research-risk-discipline | always | memo, risk, journal |

## 3. Inspectable per-framework notes

- **Buffett (BUF-P01/P03/P04/P05/P08):** wonderful-to-good quality; owner
  earnings support ~fair at 22× on 14% growth; drawdown on soft quarter with
  intact margins = patience-candidate; buy-below band set from conservative
  view × (1 − margin). Opposing: single-quarter softness could be cycle turn.
- **Munger (MUN-P02/P03/P04/P07):** ruin paths (leverage/fraud/dilution)
  designed out by net cash + candor record; incentives clean (founder pay in
  stock, no related-party flags); circle: sensors legible; opposing case
  stated better than holders would.
- **Lynch (LYN-P01/P02/P08):** stalwart, story rechecked ("repeat sensor
  replacement + service attach"); solvency clean; sell triggers pre-written
  (two quarters of volume decline).
- **Marks (MRK-P02/P04/P06):** expectations reset lower is the opportunity;
  pendulum mid-swing, not extreme — no posture tilt, just security-level work;
  pull timing caveat: convergence unknowable.
- **Druckenmiller (DRU-P01/P02/P05, tactical):** liquidity tightening +
  soft tape argue for staged entry or a 1-quarter wait trigger, NOT against
  the 4-yr thesis. Exit/reversal triggers defined separately.

## 4. Disagreement ledger (material)

- Position A (Buffett/Lynch/Marks, 4-yr): approve for deep dive; price now
  acceptable inside the band.
- Position B (Druckenmiller, tactical): wait/stage over 1 quarter; break of
  support + widening spreads would cheapen entry.
- Driving assumptions: (a) soft quarter is noise vs cycle turn; (b) liquidity
  squeeze deepens vs stabilizes.
- Horizons: 4-yr compounding vs 1-quarter entry optimization.
- Emphasized evidence: A → margins/retention/cash; B → spreads/breadth/price.
- Would resolve: next quarter volumes + spreads; pre-committed: add on
  volume confirmation, pause on covenant/attrition flags.

## 5. Mandate-bound synthesis

Status: **worthy-of-deep-dive** with staged entry (e.g. half starter inside
the band, half on volume confirmation — method illustrative, sized by mandate
cap). Invalidation: two quarters of volume/margin decline or financing
deterioration. Missing: customer concentration, insider transactions, service
attach disclosure. Journal opened per template; process to be graded
separately from outcome.
